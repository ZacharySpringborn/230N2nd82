Where We Stand at Day 39

Kevin, here is a straight read on how the listing is doing, what the rest of the park is doing, and how our price compares. All market data is from IRES MLS, pulled October 13, 2026.

Bottom Line

Buyers are finding the home online, but no one has moved on to a showing.

Blue Spruce consistently sells at roughly double Larimer County’s price per square foot, so the county isn’t our benchmark. Within the park, our price per square foot sits above what recent sales have closed at.

  • The closest sale — #88, also a 2014 at 1,152 sq ft — closed at $77,500 in 21 days. We are $6,400 above it.
  • Five of the listings that were active in August have since cut about $5,000 each.
  • The strongest position in today’s market is $79,900 — laid out with the other options in Section 6.
Activity

Seen, but no follow-through

136 online views in 39 days — but zero saves, zero shares, no showings, and no buyer or agent feedback. People are looking, then moving on without reaching out.

Price

8.3% above the twin sale

At $72.83/sq ft we are above #88 ($67.27) and above the park’s 2026 sold median ($67.27). Asking prices in the park are higher, but those homes aren’t selling.

Supply

11 homes for sale, ~1 sale every 2 months

The park has closed 5 sales in 2026. At that pace, today’s 11 listings represent roughly 20 months of inventory. The last sale in the park closed July 30 — 75 days ago.

Competition

Two homes undercut us directly

#71 (same 2014 vintage) is now $79,900, and #38A (a 2021) is $82,900. A buyer comparing the three sees ours as the most expensive. Our edge is 96 extra square feet.
